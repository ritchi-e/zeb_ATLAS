Brain regions

Expert-annotated, hierarchical segmentation of the 6 dpf larval brain. Version 2.0 is mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive (MECE): every voxel belongs to exactly one leaf region.

Registered expression patterns: transgenic lines, HCR in situ gene expression, antibody stains and cfos activity maps, each averaged across fish and aligned to the reference brain.

Single-neuron morphologies

Region-to-region connectivity

Each cell counts traced neurons whose cell body lies in the row region and that terminate in the column region (min. 10 µm of arbor in region, mapZebrain criteria). This is a projection map built from single-cell morphologies, not synapse-level connectivity.

Hover a cell for counts; click to list the neurons and send them to the viewer.
